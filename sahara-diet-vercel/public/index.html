The 10:1 Grocery Store Rule

For every packaged food item, check the nutrition label. The ratio of total carbohydrates to fiber should be 10:1 or less.

⚠️

Example: A slice of bread with 20g carbs and 1g fiber = 20:1 ratio. Leave it on the shelf. Seek 8g carbs per 1g fiber.

📈 THE GRADUAL REDUCTION LADDER
1
Step 1: Portion Halving

Cut staple size in half. If dinner includes two chapatis, reduce to exactly one for the first few weeks.

2
Step 2: Substitution Phase

Replace white polished basmati rice with higher-protein grains. Introduce brown rice, parboiled rice, or quinoa as bases for daals and sabzis.

3
Step 3: "Off-Day" Strategy

Drop chapati/naan/roti completely for most of the week, reserving it for one "off day" per week to maintain cultural joy without the metabolic burden.
